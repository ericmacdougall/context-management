# CONFIRMATORY STAGE 0 (retrieval only, no model call): DRAFT, NOT TO BE RUN

Status: DRAFT written 2026-11-16 for review by main. It is an amendment plan under the frozen pre-registration (blob a72ee4563142d45b9491f1643e238d7e72b0f851); it changes no design section of it. The test split, `held_back` and `dev2` have not been read or run for any result, and nothing in this file may be run until main replies. Everything below that is a number comes from the PILOT split (94 questions with gold, 90 content clusters) or from structural counts already recorded in the pre-registration. Source of the rows: eval claims audit (design rows 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 3.6.4, 3.7.1, 3.7.2, 3.7.3, 3.7.4, 3.7.10 of DESIGN.md), which rest only on pilot numbers.

## 1. What the stage is

One retrieval-only run on the locked TEST split (363 questions after the amendments of Section 3.1a: pilot 102, dev2 16, test 363, held_back 19), whose only purpose is to confirm or refute the nine design rows listed in Section 5 with thresholds written now. Exact in-process retriever (BM25 plus E5-small cosine, intfloat/multilingual-e5-small revision 614241f); the Elasticsearch re-run is Section 10. No LLM call, no reader, no judge, no fact extraction.

## 2. Arms (fixed now)

Run exactly these arms, with the registered settings:
1. Plain BM25 (the exact in-process BM25: k1 1.2, b 0.75, lowercase alphanumeric tokens, the 33-word stoplist, no stemming; round-level max over chunks).
2. Dense (E5-small cosine, `query: ` / `passage: ` prefixes, round-level max over chunks).
3. RRF: client-side reciprocal rank fusion of 1 and 2, rank constant 60, at the registered depth 200 (the two input lists and the output cut at 200 rounds; `emlab.runner` DEPTH = 200).
4. Cross-encoder rerank at its registered setting: `BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`, head size `RERANK_TOP_N = 20` of the RRF list, chunk-max, 512-token pair limit, the tail unchanged.
5. Packed-context presence at 4,000 and 8,000 tokens: the RRF ranking at depth 200 packed by the registered strict-prefix rule (ceil(chars/4) tokens), reported as: any gold session present, share of gold sessions present, gold round present, tokens used, and for knowledge-update questions the gold-latest session present and the stale-only rate (stale-only counts as a miss).
Secondary registered arms, run in the same pass because they are already in the pre-registration grid and cost nothing more: RRF rank constants 20 and 100, linear fusion with dense weights 0.3, 0.5, 0.7 (these exist only to test row 3.7.1, Section 5).

EXPLICITLY NOT part of this stage, with the reason:
- RRF at depth 400 or 1,000, the 50-round tier-0 list (D50), and candidate-depth ceilings (E4): exploratory, chosen after seeing pilot results; the registered curve is depth 200, and mixing definitions produced the baseline discrepancies found in the claims audit (reconciliation in EXPLORATORY.md). The RAG-context numbers of the pilot used depth 400; here the contexts use the depth-200 ranking so that one definition serves every row.
- Two-stage session-first retrieval (E2), the confidence predictor (E3), session-sum pooling, rare-term boosting, query expansion and Rocchio feedback (E5 a to c), the cross-encoder over 200 or 400 rounds (E5 d): they did not hold on the pilot (or are still pilot-only) and nothing here should depend on them.
- The analyst arms (tools, prompts, candidate lists): they need LLM calls and a run against the locked test split under its own pre-registered rule (design register item 42).
- The length shortcut as an arm or as a correction: it is a diagnostic. Length-matched and size-matched evaluations are registered SECONDARY analyses (Section 7), not arms, because the shortcut is a property of the benchmark, not of the system, and no primary conclusion may be adjusted for it after seeing test data.
- Time-aware modes (`expand_filter`, `expand_boost`) and the day-granularity as-of filter: the as-of rule is identical to `off` on S and the expand modes are exploratory.
- 16,000 and 32,000-token budgets (design row 3.6.4 quotes them): the frozen pre-registration registers 4,000 and 8,000 only; see Section 11.

## 3. Pool levels and sets

Levels exactly as registered (PREREGISTRATION 4.1): H0 = own haystack; H1 = own + D500; H2 = own + DL with L = 3,900; H3 = own + every canonical pool session whose content is in no haystack of the question's content cluster (and, by the residual rule, no session sharing a round hash with a gold round of the question). TEST-split sets are built by the registered rule for the test split: eligible = canonical sessions whose content is in no haystack of a TEST question and is gold for no question in any split (3,937 eligible, matched L = 3,900), ordered by sha256("16096:D:test:<session id>"). The population note stands: 2,877 of the 3,937 come only from pilot haystacks, so test H2 and pilot H2 draw different populations, and the test H1 draws 500 of the same pool; the stage reports the pilot and test curves side by side and does not claim they are the same population. The runner currently refuses the test split; a single dated unlock (one command-line change guarded by the freeze hash) is needed for this stage and for no other.

## 4. Denominators

- Test split: 363 questions = 350 non-abstention questions with retrieval gold + 13 abstention questions (excluded from every retrieval metric by the `_abs` rule).
- Primary denominator: the 350. Content clusters among them: computed structurally at run time (the 363 questions form 340 content clusters; the 350 are expected to span about 327). Bootstrap unit and Wilson unit: content cluster.
- Registered secondary denominator ("sensitivity analysis", PREREGISTRATION 3.1c): the 350 minus the 51 questions whose gold content sits verbatim in pilot or dev2 haystacks, i.e. 299. EVERY row below is reported on both; a conclusion that holds only on the full 350 is reported as NOT ESTABLISHED.
- Strata reported separately: the whole-pool low-overlap stratum (generator `emlab.lowoverlap_pool`), the knowledge-update questions (40 non-abstention, 40 clusters), the other types per type.
- `held_back` (19) and `dev2` (16): never in any denominator here.
- Intervals: proportions (presence, hit rates, stratum shares): Wilson 95% by content cluster (cluster rate = mean over its questions; Wilson applied to the mean of cluster rates with n = clusters). Differences between arms or levels: paired bootstrap clustered by content cluster, 2,000 resamples, seed 16096 (`clustered_paired_bootstrap`), two-sided 95% for reporting. No interval is a Wilson interval on a difference. Each of the nine rows is judged on its own with its own stated rule; the nine statements are reported together and no multiplicity adjustment is applied (they are descriptive confirmations of separate design claims, not one hypothesis family).

## 5. The nine rows: statement, pilot value and spread, and the pass or fail rule (all thresholds fixed now from the pilot)

## 6. Controls (must hold or the stage's results are void)

- Planted-gold sanity (two parts). (a) Needle: for each question a synthetic session whose single round is the question text verbatim is planted in the level's pool in memory (never written to any index); BM25 and dense must return it at round rank 1 for at least 0.95 of the 350 questions at every level, and packed-context presence at 1,000 tokens and above must be 1.00 when the gold round is placed first (the planted-gold check used for the pilot presence metric). (b) Pilot reproduction: the same harness, command and arms are run on the PILOT split first and must reproduce results/pilot/curve_pilot.json (RRF session recall@10 0.968, 0.859, 0.684, 0.4754 at H0 to H3) to 4 decimals before the test run starts, so that a harness change cannot hide behind a population change.
- Shuffled-gold floor: a seeded derangement of the 350 questions (seed 16096) assigns each question another question's gold session ids; with the true question and the true level lists, session recall@10 and any-gold presence must be at most 0.02 at H1, H2 and H3 (chance is about 10 / 18,000 at H3) and exactly 0 at H0 (the other question's gold is not in this haystack). A control that exceeds its floor voids the stage and the harness is investigated before any result is read.
- Wrong-query control for the two-arm differences (rows 3.7.1, 3.7.2, 3.7.10): the same comparison on another question's query must give a difference with an interval that includes 0 (it is expected to be exactly 0 because every arm is at the floor).
- Harness gates carried over: id guard and leak check (0 label-bearing strings), the day-rule identity (0 sessions removed), content-twin assertion in every level list.

## 7. Registered secondary analyses

- Length-matched and size-matched variants (E6 as committed in EXPLORATORY.md): the ranked list of every arm restricted to the length-matched pool (the gold sessions' length range widened by 25% on each side, gold kept), and to random same-size pools (8 seeds 16096 to 16103), with the committed survival rule (survives iff the length-matched interval excludes 0 and its mean is at least half the full-pool difference; "pool-size explained" if the random same-size difference is within 0.02 of the length-matched one). They are descriptive; no primary row's PASS or FAIL is changed by them, but a row difference that does not survive is reported as "length or pool-size explained".
- The low-overlap 23-equivalent stratum, per type breakdown, and the knowledge-update subset at levels H1, H2, H3.
- The sensitivity denominator of 299 for every row (Section 4).
- Packed-context presence at 8,000 tokens for every level (primary rows use H3 at 4,000 and 8,000 as stated).
- The pilot and test curves side by side, with the paired difference of test minus pilot at each level reported as a population-difference diagnostic (not a pass/fail).

## 8. What running it burns, and what stays

Burned by this stage on the test split (any later analysis on test that depends on these cannot be confirmatory afterwards): (i) every retrieval-level claim in the nine rows and the registered secondary analyses; the arms, depth, packing and rerank head are then frozen and cannot be re-chosen from test results; (ii) the identity of the test split's low-overlap questions and its per-question gold-in-context outcomes: any later Phase F or analyst design that uses them (choosing candidate depth, budgets, which strata to analyse, or confidence thresholds) is no longer blind on this split; (iii) the test-split values of baselines used as the comparison arm in later confirmatory tests: the later analyst-versus-RRF and facts-versus-baselines comparisons on the test split must use the arms and definitions fixed here (depth 200 RRF, rerank top 20, budgets 4,000 and 8,000). NOT burned: the answers, the judge, any fact extraction, and the K1/K2 decisions: no answer-level or fact-level quantity is observed here, so those confirmatory tests remain possible, but they must state that retrieval outcomes on the test split were seen. Stays for `held_back`: it is never confirmatory; it may be used after this stage for any exploratory or tuning work that would otherwise burn the test split (for example the analyst candidate-order shuffle, threshold choices, the 16,000 and 32,000 budget grid, depth 400 variants); it contains content twins of pilot questions, so results on it are exploratory by construction. `dev2` stays the fact-linking gate.

## 9. Compute estimate (loaded shared host, exact retriever)

From the pilot (host load 17 to 55; per question mean seconds): retrieval at H0 0.23, H1 0.53, H2 1.22, H3 4.3 for BM25, dense and RRF together; the pilot curve of 94 questions took about 13 minutes including loading. For 350 questions: about 50 minutes for the retrieval curve plus about 10 minutes of loading, about 40 minutes for the length-matched and size-matched variants (8 seeds), about 10 minutes of context packing and the low-overlap generator, about 15 minutes for the controls and the pilot reproduction. The cross-encoder dominates: about 20 to 27 s per question and level (measured over the 94-question run, scores cached across levels, ~90 s per question over four levels), so about 8.5 hours for 350 questions on the loaded host, about 4 to 5 hours on a quiet host (estimate, not measured). Total on the loaded host: roughly 10 to 11 hours of wall time at low priority, run detached with a stop-file; CPU only, 12 threads at most, no GPU, no model API. Disk: about 1 GB for the result files and rankings.

## 10. What must be re-run on Elasticsearch for the Basic-licence target

Nothing in this stage is an Elasticsearch result (pilot round-level parity of the exact pipeline: Elasticsearch BM25 shares 0.751 of the exact top 10; client RRF against Elasticsearch `rrf` 0.817). Before any recall figure is quoted for the Basic target (design register item 44): re-run arms 1 to 5 end to end on Elasticsearch 9.5 with Basic features only: BM25 `match` on the english-analyzed `text` field, kNN on `vector` (bbq_hnsw, num_candidates 1,000, `rescore_vector` oversample 3, the filter heuristic read back), client-side RRF 60 at depth 200, the same cross-encoder; filters by the level's session ids (H3 as a `must_not` of the excluded sessions); report for each row the Elasticsearch value, the exact-retriever value and their paired difference, and apply the same PASS and FAIL rules to the Elasticsearch numbers separately. Compute is small (the index `emlab-rounds-v1` already holds the chunks; queries take milliseconds); the cross-encoder cost repeats unless the same candidate lists are reused. A semantic_text deployment that does not add the `passage:` and `query:` prefixes changes rankings materially (top-10 overlap 0.718) and is out of scope.

## 11. What would need a NEW pre-registration (not allowed by the frozen one)

Quoting the 16,000 and 32,000-token presence figures of row 3.6.4 (only 4,000 and 8,000 are registered); any depth other than 200 (for example 400 or 1,000) or a deeper rerank head; the analyst arms on the test split with shuffled candidate order (item 42) and their own rule; the confidence predictor, two-stage retrieval or any first-stage variant as an arm; a correction or reweighting for the length shortcut as a primary analysis; the time-aware expand modes as hypotheses; thresholds for the answer-level K1 and K2 (those need pilot deltas and the user's acceptance in their own words); any use of `held_back` or `dev2` as confirmatory data; an Elasticsearch-based primary analysis in place of the exact retriever; a change of the whole-pool low-overlap threshold (100) or of the sensitivity-analysis rule; anything that depends on the test split's retrieval outcomes (Section 8) being treated as blind.
