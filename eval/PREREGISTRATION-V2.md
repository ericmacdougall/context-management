## 1. Parts, in the order they run, and what each part may and may not know

The order is fixed here so that no part's outcome can leak into a later part's design. Rule L (leak rule): every arm, prompt, depth, budget, threshold and stopping rule of every part is frozen in V2 BEFORE any part runs on the test split; no test outcome of an earlier part may change any of them; the only dependencies allowed between parts are the pre-registered gates of Section 1.2, which are evaluated on PILOT data only (never on test or held_back results).

- G-extraction: the typed-state and facts arms (parts 4 and 5) run only if the extraction-and-linking pilot meets the bar of S1; otherwise those arms are reported as not run (K1/K2 undecided, as now).
- G-parts: a failed control in part 0 voids every later test part until fixed and re-run.

## 3. Part R: retrieval-only confirmation (no model calls)

R1. The nine Stage 0 rows (1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 3.6.4, 3.7.1, 3.7.2, 3.7.3, 3.7.4, 3.7.10) with exactly the arms, thresholds, controls and PASS and FAIL rules of CONFIRMATORY-STAGE0.md Section 5 and 6 (thresholds already set from the pilot spread there). Their polarity stays: PASS = the stated limitation or pilot effect is confirmed.
R2. Depth. Hypothesis: a deeper candidate list raises the share of low-overlap questions that have any gold in the list. Metric: HIT@400 minus HIT@50 (rounds, flat RRF, depth-1,000 inputs cut at K) on the whole-pool low-overlap stratum of the test split. Pilot: HIT@50 0.30, HIT@400 0.65 (difference +0.35; n 23, Wilson half-width about 0.18; all 94: 0.79 to 0.92). Threshold (set from the pilot spread, written now): PASS iff the difference is at least +0.20 with the paired lower bound above 0; FAIL iff the upper bound is below +0.10. Control: the same difference with another question's query must have an interval including 0 (both are at the floor). Descriptive companions: HIT@100, 200, 1,000 and session recall@K (E4 definitions).
R3. Deeper rerank head. Hypothesis: a cross-encoder head of 200 (then 400) rounds raises session recall@10 on the low-overlap stratum over flat RRF. Metric: paired mean difference in session recall@10 (cross-encoder over the top K, K in {200, 400}, minus flat RRF) on the whole-pool low-overlap stratum. Threshold (fixed from the finished E5(d) pilot, 2026-11-16): the pilot (23 pilot low-overlap questions, K = 200, about 345 pairs per question on average, about 243 s per question) gave flat RRF 0.041, cross-encoder over the top 200 0.081, paired gain +0.041 [-0.020, +0.114]; the wrong-query control gave 0.000 for both (no gain credited); gold was among the 200 candidates for 11 of 23 questions; the planned +0.05 rule with a lower bound above 0 was NOT met, so K = 400 was not run. R3 is therefore a registered NEGATIVE hypothesis: PASS = no gain of practical size (upper paired bound below +0.05 at K = 200; K = 400 is run only if the K = 200 upper bound is at or above +0.05). The +0.03 bound first drafted is not used: at the test's low-overlap size the expected interval half-width is about 0.036 (Section 13), so a true gain of exactly 0 would have an upper bound of about +0.036 and could not confirm a +0.03 bound, with the extra cost per question reported; whether about 80 test low-overlap questions resolve a +/-0.03 interval is NOT established by this pilot (23 questions, interval width 0.13) and needs the V2 power check. Cost: about 1.3 s per round on the loaded host (200 rounds about 260 to 310 s per question, 23 questions) which is why R3 runs on the low-overlap stratum only (about 80 questions at the test's 0.245 share, expected from the pilot).
R4. Registered secondaries (descriptive; none changes a PASS or FAIL): length-matched and size-matched analyses (8 seeds 16096 to 16103) for R1 to R3; the sensitivity denominator; the pilot-versus-test curves side by side; the RRF constants 20 and 100 and the linear weights; the 8,000-token presence.
Controls for all of R: planted needle, gold-first presence, shuffled-gold floor, wrong-query, pilot reproduction (Stage 0 Section 6).
Calls: 0 model calls. Compute: about 10 to 11 hours of wall time on the loaded host for R1 (Stage 0 Section 9) plus about 1.5 hours for R2 and about 6 hours for R3 on the low-overlap stratum (estimate: about 80 questions x 280 s).

## 13. Power check: minimum detectable differences at the test split's size (planning figures, not guarantees)

Method: pilot interval half-widths (95%, content-cluster bootstrap) scaled by sqrt(n_pilot / n_test), assuming the same per-question spread and about one cluster per question; the difference that 80% power at a two-sided 5% level needs is about 1.43 times the expected half-width (2.8 / 1.96). Test sizes: 350 questions, low-overlap about 80 (0.245 share), knowledge-update 40, the part K set 350. These are rough: the pilot spreads come from 23 to 94 questions and are themselves uncertain; a real power calculation from the per-question pilot differences replaces this before V2 is frozen.
| Comparison (pilot source) | Pilot half-width (n) | Expected test half-width | Minimum detectable difference (80% power) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-REORDER minus cross-encoder, all questions, recall@10 (Stage G) | 0.069 (94) | about 0.036 (350) | about 0.05 |
| A-REORDER minus RRF, low-overlap, recall@10 (Stage G) | 0.124 (23) | about 0.066 (80) | about 0.09 to 0.10 |
| R3 cross-encoder at 200 minus flat RRF, low-overlap (E5(d)) | 0.067 (23) | about 0.036 (80) | about 0.05 |

| ORACLE minus RRF, judged accuracy (Stage G part B) | 0.114 (77) | about 0.053 (350) | about 0.08 |

Consequences registered now: (1) every low-overlap comparison at the test's size cannot resolve a true difference smaller than about 0.05 to 0.10 in recall@10 (about 0.13 in judged accuracy); a low-overlap result inside that band is reported as unresolved, never as a null; (2) negative-hypothesis rules therefore use an upper-bound line of +0.05 (R3, A-2), not +0.03; (3) the knowledge-update stratum (40 questions, about 35 clusters) has a half-width of the order of 0.15 for any contrast of proportions, so only differences of about 0.2 or more are resolvable there; (4) part X's weak-list subset (Section 14) is about 11 to 12 percent of the questions, about 40 test questions, resolving only gains of about 0.2 in a share of questions.

## 14. Part X: typed facts and explicit links as candidate generation (MODEL CALLS; WAITING for the user's approval of extraction volume; nothing here has run)

Why: in the Stage G pilot the questions whose 300-candidate list held no gold session (11 of 94; 10 of the 91 ok ones) scored 0.000 for every arm (A-DIG, A-REORDER, RRF, the cross-encoder), and 7 of 10 dug without finding gold. A reorderer cannot place a session that is not in the list, and prompted digging did not repair it. The only route not yet tested is to change how candidates are generated: from extracted typed facts (entity, attribute, value, valid_from, source session and round) and explicit links between facts and sessions (same slot, supersedes, same entity, same event), searched in addition to the raw chunks.
Hypothesis X-1: adding fact-and-link candidates to the RRF 300 candidates (union capped at 300 total; the fact candidates take at most 100 slots, a fixed rule written before any run) raises HIT@300 (any gold session in the candidate list) on the whole-pool low-overlap and weak-list questions at H3, without lowering HIT@300 on the others. Primary metric: paired difference in HIT@300 (union minus RRF 300) on the questions with no gold among the RRF 300 candidates (the "weak-list" set, defined by the gold labels as an analysis stratum only; no arm sees it) and on the 23 to 80 low-overlap questions; secondary: session recall@10 after the A-REORDER step over the union (needs part A's model calls on top).
Extraction settings (pilot evidence, STAGE-X0.md and STAGE-X0B.md; not frozen): prompt `extract-v2-anon` with the fact cap raised from 12 to 30 (version `extract-v2-cap30`, sha16 `ae055bca99452fb6`, one number changed), one session per call, one pass. Evidence: in Stage X0 28 percent of sessions (49 percent of gold sessions) hit the cap of 12; in the 10-session cap-30 check all 10 capped sessions returned more than 12 facts (13 to 24, none at 30) at about +9 percent cost per capped call and about +1.5 percent over the pool (direction only; 10 sessions give no rate); 92.5 percent of the original 12 facts reappeared by quote match, the cap and run-to-run sampling confounded. One pass is the pilot default; whether one pass suffices is decided by X-G5, not assumed. Reading of the two Stage X0 answers missing from the facts (no-model, pilot gold): both were heuristic artefacts (a count across five sessions; a paraphrased preference rubric), none was a cap effect; one small prompt gap was found (the user's stated wish list is under-extracted against assistant-stated details), so a pilot prompt revision that asks for the user's stated preferences and interest lists is to be tried and re-gated, as a NEW version string, before volume. The answer-string heuristic is not a gate for count and preference questions; part X is gated on HIT@300 of the gold session, not on strings.
Arms: RRF 300 (baseline); union with facts (X); union with facts but links removed (X-nolink, tests the links); union with randomly drawn facts of the same count (X-random control); union with facts retrieved for another question's text (wrong-query control, must show no gain). All candidate texts are cut and opaque-id'd as in Stage G; the extraction prompts carry no question and no gold information.
Pilot design (pilot split; the gate set below is separate): X-a (H1, extraction quality): extraction for the pilot's own haystacks plus D500 (about 5,100 unique sessions at one session per call) plus linking (about 1,000 calls) = about 6,100 calls, to measure fact precision and link correctness. X-b (H3, candidate generation): needs facts for the WHOLE deduplicated pool, 18,464 unique contents at one per call, about 18,464 extraction calls (the 5,100 of X-a are part of it) plus about 4,000 linking calls, so about 22,500 calls in total; this one pool extraction serves the pilot and, unchanged, the test (the test sessions are in the same pool), so the pilot and test volumes are one cost, not two. A cheaper X-b is not valid: extracting only the sessions a raw retriever already ranked high would reproduce the candidates RRF already has. Extraction time for this volume is not measured and is to be estimated from a canary before approval.
Fresh gate set it needs (all must pass BEFORE X-b or any test run): neither dev2 (already used once: 10 of 16 updates linked against a bar of 12, 9 of 25 linked pairs flipped between two batchings) nor held_back (exploratory) may be the gate, and the 11 Stage G weak-list pilot questions are now seen. A fresh gate set of 30 questions (15 low-overlap-like, 15 knowledge-update) must be reserved by a NEW hash-guarded split rule, drawn before the test split is unlocked and removed from every confirmatory denominator (main's decision; listed in Section 11). Gates: X-G1 fact precision (at least 0.90 on 100 facts human-audited by the user, lower Wilson bound at least 0.80); X-G2 link correctness (at least 12 of 16 true updates linked, 0 false supersessions in 16, and at most 10 percent of linked pairs changing between two batchings); X-G3 provenance and leak checks (every fact has a source session and round; the id guard passes; no gold or question text in any extraction prompt; a planted-fact check retrieves a planted fact by its value); X-G4 on the gate set, HIT@300 of X at least 0.10 above RRF 300 with the wrong-query control at 0; X-G5 extraction stability (new, from Stage X0B): an identical-prompt replicate (same prompt, same sessions, a second run) of at least 20 gate-set sessions must reproduce at least 85 percent of the first run's facts by quote match (normalised quotes equal, or one contains the other with the shorter at least 8 characters), and the facts of the replicate must pass the quote-and-speaker check at a rate of at least 0.95 on 100 or more facts; if X-G5 fails, a second pass or a verification step over the same session is added and re-gated before any volume. The X-1 threshold on the test split is TO FIX FROM PILOT (X-b) and is not written until X-b exists; with about 40 weak-list questions only a gain of about 0.2 is resolvable (Section 13).
Calls (cap-30 repricing of Stage X0: about +1.5 percent output cost over the pool, so the totals below stand to that accuracy; the X-G5 replicate adds at least 20 calls): pilot about 6,100 (X-a) plus about 22,500 in total with X-b; test: no new extraction (same pool), plus A-REORDER reorder calls over the union (350 at H3) if the secondary metric is run.
