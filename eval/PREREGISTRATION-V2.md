## 1. Parts, in the order they run, and what each part may and may not know

The order is fixed here so that no part's outcome can leak into a later part's design. Rule L (leak rule): every arm, prompt, depth, budget, threshold and stopping rule of every part is frozen in V2 BEFORE any part runs on the test split; no test outcome of an earlier part may change any of them; the only dependencies allowed between parts are the pre-registered gates of Section 1.2, which are evaluated on PILOT data only (never on test or held_back results).

| P | Further pilots that set thresholds (Section 4) | judge validation, headroom, analyst re-pilot, extraction and typed-state pilot, Elasticsearch end-to-end pilot, deeper-rerank pilot (the last is already done as exploratory E5(d)) | yes, except the Elasticsearch pilot | pilot (and dev2 where stated), never test or held_back as confirmatory |

Why this order: retrieval-only parts first because they cost no model call and their outcomes (which questions are low-overlap, where gold is) must not steer the model parts; part 2 after part 1 so that Elasticsearch numbers cannot change arm definitions; the analyst arms before the answer-accuracy parts because their contexts are inputs to part 5 (arms that use them are fixed by Section 8 in advance); the typed state arm before part 5 for the same reason. Parts 3 to 5 are independent of one another's test outcomes: each one's contexts are produced from the frozen arms, and part 5 may reuse parts 3 and 4's context files verbatim (no regeneration after seeing accuracy).

### 1.1 Approval and stopping

Parts 3, 4, 5 and every pilot marked MODEL CALLS wait for the user's approval, stage by stage; V2 states the estimated calls (Section 9) so the approval is informed; an approval for one stage is not approval for the next. Parts 0, 1, 2 need no approval beyond main's unlock of the test split (one dated, hash-guarded command).

### 1.2 Pre-registered gates (evaluated on pilot data only)

- G-judge (before any answer-level run): the judge-validation pilot (Section 4, J1) must meet its bars; if it does not, parts 5 and the answer-level pilots do not run and the stage is reported blocked.
- G-headroom: the pilot headroom test (J2) must show accuracy headroom for a memory layer (gold-only reader minus plain RAG reader of at least +0.10 with a paired lower bound above 0 at H3); if it does not, part 5 is reported as a cost and latency comparison only, with no accuracy hypothesis tested.
- G-extraction: the typed-state and facts arms (parts 4 and 5) run only if the extraction-and-linking pilot meets the bar of S1; otherwise those arms are reported as not run (K1/K2 undecided, as now).
- G-parts: a failed control in part 0 voids every later test part until fixed and re-run.

## 3. Part R: retrieval-only confirmation (no model calls)

R1. The nine Stage 0 rows (1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 3.6.4, 3.7.1, 3.7.2, 3.7.3, 3.7.4, 3.7.10) with exactly the arms, thresholds, controls and PASS and FAIL rules of CONFIRMATORY-STAGE0.md Section 5 and 6 (thresholds already set from the pilot spread there). Their polarity stays: PASS = the stated limitation or pilot effect is confirmed.
R2. Depth. Hypothesis: a deeper candidate list raises the share of low-overlap questions that have any gold in the list. Metric: HIT@400 minus HIT@50 (rounds, flat RRF, depth-1,000 inputs cut at K) on the whole-pool low-overlap stratum of the test split. Pilot: HIT@50 0.30, HIT@400 0.65 (difference +0.35; n 23, Wilson half-width about 0.18; all 94: 0.79 to 0.92). Threshold (set from the pilot spread, written now): PASS iff the difference is at least +0.20 with the paired lower bound above 0; FAIL iff the upper bound is below +0.10. Control: the same difference with another question's query must have an interval including 0 (both are at the floor). Descriptive companions: HIT@100, 200, 1,000 and session recall@K (E4 definitions).
R3. Deeper rerank head. Hypothesis: a cross-encoder head of 200 (then 400) rounds raises session recall@10 on the low-overlap stratum over flat RRF. Metric: paired mean difference in session recall@10 (cross-encoder over the top K, K in {200, 400}, minus flat RRF) on the whole-pool low-overlap stratum. Threshold (fixed from the finished E5(d) pilot, 2026-11-16): the pilot (23 pilot low-overlap questions, K = 200, about 345 pairs per question on average, about 243 s per question) gave flat RRF 0.041, cross-encoder over the top 200 0.081, paired gain +0.041 [-0.020, +0.114]; the wrong-query control gave 0.000 for both (no gain credited); gold was among the 200 candidates for 11 of 23 questions; the planned +0.05 rule with a lower bound above 0 was NOT met, so K = 400 was not run. R3 is therefore a registered NEGATIVE hypothesis: PASS = no gain larger than +0.03 (upper paired bound below +0.03 at K = 200; K = 400 is run only if the K = 200 upper bound is at or above +0.05), with the extra cost per question reported; whether about 80 test low-overlap questions resolve a +/-0.03 interval is NOT established by this pilot (23 questions, interval width 0.13) and needs the V2 power check. Cost: about 1.3 s per round on the loaded host (200 rounds about 260 to 310 s per question, 23 questions) which is why R3 runs on the low-overlap stratum only (about 80 questions at the test's 0.245 share, expected from the pilot).
R4. Registered secondaries (descriptive; none changes a PASS or FAIL): length-matched and size-matched analyses (8 seeds 16096 to 16103) for R1 to R3; the sensitivity denominator; the pilot-versus-test curves side by side; the RRF constants 20 and 100 and the linear weights; the 8,000-token presence.
Controls for all of R: planted needle, gold-first presence, shuffled-gold floor, wrong-query, pilot reproduction (Stage 0 Section 6).
Calls: 0 model calls. Compute: about 10 to 11 hours of wall time on the loaded host for R1 (Stage 0 Section 9) plus about 1.5 hours for R2 and about 6 hours for R3 on the low-overlap stratum (estimate: about 80 questions x 280 s).

## 6. Part E: Elasticsearch Basic replication (no model calls)

Hypothesis: the conclusions of part R hold on the Basic-licence target. Pipeline: Elasticsearch 9.5 Basic features only: `match` on the english-analyzed `text`, kNN on `vector` (bbq_hnsw, num_candidates 1,000, `rescore_vector` oversample 3, filter heuristic read back), client-side RRF 60 at depth 200, the same cross-encoder, filters by the level's session ids (H3 as `must_not` of the excluded sessions), the index `emlab-rounds-v1` rebuilt or verified identical (document count 150,526 and chunk hashes). Primary metric: for each of BM25, dense, RRF and the cross-encoder at each level, the Elasticsearch session recall@10 and its paired difference to the exact retriever. Parity rule: TO FIX FROM PILOT (ES-pilot above, 0 model calls, minutes). Evidence for the shape of the rule: round-level top-10 overlap with the exact retriever on the pilot was 0.751 for BM25 and 0.817 for RRF; the end-metric loss of an Elasticsearch dense leg without rescoring was up to 0.078 at H2 and 0.041 at H3, and at most 0.018 and 0.014 with oversample 3 on bbq. Planned rule (to be confirmed or changed by ES-pilot before freezing): replication HOLDS for an arm and level iff the paired mean difference to the exact retriever lies within +/-0.03 (interval inside +/-0.05); the PASS and FAIL rules of part R's nine rows are then re-evaluated on the Elasticsearch numbers separately and reported next to the exact-retriever verdicts. Controls: the heuristic read-back, the wrong-query control, a planted needle through Elasticsearch, a node-health check, and the prefix check of the E5 endpoint (not used: vectors are external). Denominators: the 350 and the 299. Calls: 0. Compute: queries take milliseconds; the cross-encoder repeats unless the same candidate lists are reused (they are reused where the Elasticsearch candidate list equals the exact one; otherwise rescored).

## 7. Part A: analyst arms (MODEL CALLS)

Hypotheses (retrieval-level, no answers): A-1 an analyst raises session recall@10 over the cross-encoder rerank at the whole pool; A-2 digging with tools raises session recall@10 over reordering the same candidates on the whole-pool low-overlap stratum; A-3 the analyst raises the current-value presence for knowledge-update questions at H3, 4,000 tokens.
Design changes from the pilot run (all registered here): candidate ORDER IS SHUFFLED (a seeded permutation per question and level, seed 16096, written into the task file; the original tier-0 rank is not shown), so anchoring on the tier-0 order is removed and the candidate count stays 50 (20 as a secondary variant); the prompt requires digging when the candidates look weak (final text to be fixed from pilot A1 and registered with its version string before freezing; the rule it implements: "if none of the candidates contains evidence that answers the question, you must use at least [m] tool calls before answering"; the neutral wording of the frozen prompt is otherwise kept, no mention of level or pool size); the tool daemon's budget is keyed on (question, level, variant) (fixed in code); tier-0 depth stays 50 rounds and the baseline of every comparison is reported against BOTH the tier-0 candidates and the registered curve (depth 200).
Primary metrics and thresholds (pilot spread): A-1: paired mean difference ANALYST minus cross-encoder in session recall@10 at H3 on all 350: pilot +0.075 [+0.012, +0.137] (94 questions, unshuffled order, old prompt). Threshold: PASS iff the mean difference is at least +0.04 with the paired lower bound above 0; FAIL iff the upper bound is below +0.02. TO FIX FROM PILOT: the threshold is provisional until A1 re-runs the pilot with shuffled order and the digging prompt (the anchoring control can change the effect; set from A1 before freezing). A-2: ANALYST minus ANALYST-RERANK-ONLY on the low-overlap stratum at H3: pilot -0.020 [-0.104, +0.065] (n 23, old prompt): TO FIX FROM PILOT (A1; planned rule: PASS iff the mean difference is at least +0.05 with a lower bound above 0, FAIL iff the upper bound is below +0.03). A-3: gold-latest presence for the 40 knowledge-update questions at H3, 4,000 tokens, analyst minus cross-encoder: pilot analyst 0.93, cross-encoder 0.80, RRF 0.33 (n 15; differences among the reordering arms unresolvable): descriptive only (no pass rule) until A1 sets one.
Controls: (i) shuffled-order control (a pilot control, not a test run): the unshuffled and shuffled analyst agree within the pilot noise before the shuffled arm is used; (ii) wrong-candidates control: an analyst given another question's 50 candidates (same level) must return session recall@10 at the floor (at most 0.05), run on 30 test questions; (iii) no-tool control: the analyst with tools disabled is ANALYST-RERANK-ONLY (already an arm); (iv) planted needle: a session with the verbatim question text planted in a tool-visible level scope must be retrieved by the analyst in at least 0.95 of 30 planted questions (confirms the tools can find a planted answer); (v) rejection handling: rejected files are failures and stay in every denominator, with ok-only beside them, as registered.
Denominators: 350 (299 sensitivity), low-overlap stratum separately, knowledge-update 40; levels H2 and H3 primary, H1 secondary.
Calls on test: 350 questions x {ANALYST, ANALYST-RERANK-ONLY} x {H2, H3} = 1,400 calls (700 analyst calls of about 100,000 tokens and 22 s each, so about 70 million input tokens and about 4.3 hours of analyst time at concurrency 1, about 1.5 hours at concurrency 3; 700 rerank-only calls of about 31,000 tokens and 10 s) plus 60 control calls; H1 adds 700 calls if approved. Pilot A1: 376 calls (Section 4).
Registered secondaries: length-matched and size-matched analyses of A-1 and A-2 (the survival rule), the 20-candidate variant on the low-overlap stratum, tool use per question (zero-tool share).

## 8. Part S: typed current-state arm for knowledge-update questions (MODEL CALLS)

Hypothesis: a typed current-state context (the current value of each slot, its source quote and date, from a facts layer with valid_from and a linking step) puts the CURRENT value in the 4,000-token context for more knowledge-update questions than plain search and as many as the analyst, and rarely only the stale value. Arm S: a context made of the typed current-state rows for the question's slots (a decision or fact is current only by its recorded supersession; lower trust never supersedes higher; each row carries its source quote) followed by the plain RAG fill to the same 4,000 tokens, deduplicated by source round; the same packing rule and chronological ordering as every other arm; the state table is built from extraction at one session per call on the question's own haystack (H0 and H1 only, because facts exist only for those sessions) and linked by the existing linking step.
Primary metric: gold-latest present in the context (stale-only counted as a miss) on the 40 knowledge-update non-abstention test questions at H1 (the pooled levels have no facts for distractors). Comparators: RAG (RRF 4,000), the cross-encoder arm, the analyst arm at H1. Pilot values for the shape: knowledge-update at H1 4,000 tokens: RRF 0.867 latest / 0.133 stale-only (n 15), analyst 1.00, cross-encoder 1.00; at H3: RRF 0.33, cross-encoder 0.80, analyst 0.93. Threshold: TO FIX FROM PILOT (S1). Reason: the linking step failed its dev2 gate (10 of 16 true updates linked against a bar of 12, and 9 of 25 linked pairs flipped between two batchings), so the arm has no measured operating point; no threshold may be written before a pilot with the repaired extraction and a fresh gate set (Section 4). The pilot needs about 800 extraction calls and about 200 linking calls on the 16 pilot knowledge-update questions plus the fresh gate set; it also needs the user's decision on extraction volume (the extraction hold). Controls: planted-gold (gold-first presence), a shuffled-slot control (state rows assigned to another question must give gold-latest presence at the floor), and the `false change` rate on 40 knowledge-update questions with no real update (needs a negative set: held_back or generated, exploratory only; confirmatory control = the abstention-twin questions, which have no update). Denominator: 40 (and the sensitivity subset: 0 of the 51 excluded questions are knowledge-update, so no change). Calls on test: extraction for 40 questions x 48 sessions (about 1,900 unique sessions) plus linking, about 2,100 calls, and the arm costs no further model call (retrieval-level scoring). If part 5 reuses the context, no regeneration.
